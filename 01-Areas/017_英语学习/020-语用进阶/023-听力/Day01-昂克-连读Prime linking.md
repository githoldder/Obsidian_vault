## 核心规则
辅音结尾 + 元音开头 → 连读成一个音节

**not at all → no-ta-tall**
**pick up → pi-kup**
**look at → loo-kat**
**an apple → a-napple**

### 特殊：/r/ /l/ /ŋ/ 双写
**for a → for-ra**
**all of → al-lof**
**sing a → sing-ga**
