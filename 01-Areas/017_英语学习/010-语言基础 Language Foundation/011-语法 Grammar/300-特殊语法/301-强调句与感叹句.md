# 强调句与感叹句

**核心概括**：强调句通过特定结构突出句子中的某个成分，使其成为信息焦点；感叹句用来表达强烈的情感，以 what 或 how 引导。

> [!info] 语法规则
> - **It is/was... that/who...** 强调句结构：可以强调主语、宾语、状语，但**不能强调谓语**
> - **do/does/did + 动词原形**：强调谓语动词
> - **感叹句**：What + (a/an) + adj. + n. + 主谓！ / How + adj./adv. + 主谓！

---

## 一、It is/was... that/who... 强调结构

### 1.1 基本结构

将要强调的成分放在 It is/was 和 that/who 之间，其余部分放在 that/who 之后。

**原句**：Tom met Mary at the park yesterday.

| 强调对象 | 强调句 |
|----------|--------|
| 主语 Tom | **It was Tom** that/who met Mary at the park yesterday. |
| 宾语 Mary | **It was Mary** that/whom Tom met at the park yesterday. |
| 地点状语 | **It was at the park** that Tom met Mary yesterday. |
| 时间状语 | **It was yesterday** that Tom met Mary at the park. |

**例句**：
1. **It was** my mother **who** taught me to be honest. （简单 - 强调主语）
2. **It was** not until midnight **that** he finished all his homework. （中等 - 强调时间状语 not until）
3. **It was** precisely because the researchers had adopted an interdisciplinary approach combining data science with traditional sociological methods **that** they were able to identify patterns that had eluded previous investigators for decades. （复杂 - 强调原因状语）

### 1.2 强调句的判断方法

> [!info] 语法规则
> **去掉 It is/was 和 that/who，剩余部分如果能构成一个完整的句子，则为强调句。**

**判断练习**：
- **It was** Tom **that** met Mary yesterday. → 去掉后：Tom met Mary yesterday.（完整句子 → 强调句）
- **It** is important **that** we study hard. → 去掉后：important we study hard.（不完整 → 形式主语句）

### 1.3 强调句的特殊形式

| 形式 | 结构 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| 一般疑问句 | Is/Was it... that...? | **Was it** Tom **that** broke the window? |
| 特殊疑问句 | 疑问词 + is/was it that...? | **Who was it that** broke the window? |
| not until 强调 | It is/was not until... that... | **It was not until** he failed **that** he realized the importance of effort. |

**例句**：
1. **Was it** you **who** called me last night? （简单 - 疑问形式）
2. **Where was it that** you first met your best friend? （中等 - 特殊疑问形式）
3. **It was not until** the investigation had been completed and the full extent of the damage had been assessed **that** the authorities finally acknowledged the severity of the crisis and began to take the decisive action **that** the public had been demanding for months. （复杂 - not until 强调句）

> [!warning] 易错提醒
> - 强调句**不能强调谓语动词**：不能说 ~~It was met that Tom Mary yesterday.~~
> - 强调谓语要用 **do/does/did** 结构（见下节）
> - 强调句中被强调部分是人时，可用 who/that；是物/时间/地点时，只能用 that
> - not until 放入强调句时，主句**不倒装**：It was not until... **that he realized**...（正常语序）

## 二、do/does/did 强调谓语

当需要强调**谓语动词**时，在动词原形前加 do/does/did。

**例句**：
1. I **do** love you. （简单 - 强调"确实"爱）
2. She **did** finish the project on time, despite all the obstacles. （中等 - did + 动词原形）
3. The government **does** recognize the urgency of the situation and **did** allocate additional funds last quarter, but critics argue that these measures **do** not go far enough to address the structural problems that continue to undermine economic stability. （复杂 - 多个 do/does/did 强调）

> [!warning] 易错提醒
> - do/does/did 后面必须接**动词原形**：
>   - She **does like** it.（正确）
>   - ~~She **does likes** it.~~（错误）
> - 这种强调只用于**肯定句和祈使句**，不用于疑问句和否定句

## 三、感叹句

### 3.1 What 引导的感叹句

**结构**：
- What + **a/an + adj. + 可数名词单数** + 主语 + 谓语！
- What + **adj. + 不可数名词/可数名词复数** + 主语 + 谓语！

**例句**：
1. **What a beautiful** day (it is)! （简单 - 可数名词单数）
2. **What interesting** books they are! （中等 - 可数名词复数，不加 a/an）
3. **What an extraordinary** achievement it is for a team of undergraduate students to have developed a working prototype that has attracted the attention of leading technology companies from around the world! （复杂）

### 3.2 How 引导的感叹句

**结构**：
- How + **adj./adv.** + 主语 + 谓语！
- How + 主语 + 谓语！（强调整个句子）

**例句**：
1. **How beautiful** she is! （简单 - how + 形容词）
2. **How quickly** time flies when you are having fun! （中等 - how + 副词）
3. **How remarkable** it is that a single discovery made in a small laboratory could ultimately transform the way millions of people around the world understand and treat a disease that had been considered incurable for centuries! （复杂）

### 3.3 What vs. How 的选择

| | What | How |
|---|------|-----|
| 后接 | 名词（短语） | 形容词/副词 |
| 判断 | 感叹的核心词是名词 | 感叹的核心词是形容词/副词 |
| 转换 | What a clever boy! | How clever the boy is! |

> [!tip] 实战技巧
> **阅读中**：
> - 遇到 It is/was... that 结构，先用"去掉法"判断是强调句还是形式主语
> - 强调句突出的部分往往是句子的信息焦点，理解它有助于把握作者意图
>
> **写作中**：
> - 用强调句突出关键信息：It is this approach that makes the difference.
> - 用 do/does/did 在议论文中加强语气：The evidence does support this conclusion.
> - 学术写作中慎用感叹句，主要用于演讲稿和非正式文体
