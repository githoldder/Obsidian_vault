# Day03 昂克英文君：连读 Geminates（辅音+辅音）

## 视频
[【最全英语连读规则】(03 连读 Geminates 辅音+辅音)](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1jE41157bX?p=3)

## 核心规则

### 相同辅音，只读一个
**big girl → bi-girl**
**good day → goo-day**
**some milk → so-milk**

### 不同辅音，保留强音
**strong coffee → stron-coffee**
**next day → nex-day**

### /t/ + /j/ → /tʃ/
**meet you → mee-chu**
**don't you → don-chu**

### /d/ + /j/ → /dʒ/
**would you → wou-ju**
**did you → di-ju**
