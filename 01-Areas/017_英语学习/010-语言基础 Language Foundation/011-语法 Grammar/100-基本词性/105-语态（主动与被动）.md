# 语态（主动与被动）

**核心概括**：英语有两种语态——主动语态表示主语是动作的执行者，被动语态表示主语是动作的承受者；被动语态的核心结构是 **be + 过去分词（done）**。

> [!info] 语法规则
> - **主动语态**：主语 → 发出动作 → 宾语（He wrote the letter.）
> - **被动语态**：主语 → 接受动作 → by + 动作发出者（The letter was written by him.）
> - 只有**及物动词**才能变为被动语态（不及物动词没有宾语，无法变被动）
> - 被动语态公式：**be + 过去分词（done）**，通过改变 be 的形式体现不同时态

---

## 一、主动语态变被动语态的步骤

> [!info] 语法规则
> 1. 把主动句的**宾语**变为被动句的**主语**
> 2. 动词变为 **be + done** 形式
> 3. 主动句的**主语**变为 by + 宾格（通常可省略）

**示意图**：
```
主动：Tom    wrote   the letter.
       ↓               ↓
被动：The letter  was written  (by Tom).
```

**例句**：
1. 主动：She **cleans** the room. → 被动：The room **is cleaned** (by her). （简单）
2. 主动：People **are building** a new bridge. → 被动：A new bridge **is being built**. （中等 - 进行时被动）
3. 主动：The committee **had approved** the proposal before the scandal **was exposed**. → 被动：The proposal **had been approved** by the committee before the scandal **was exposed**. （复杂 - 过去完成时被动）

---

## 二、各时态的被动形式

| 时态 | 主动形式 | 被动形式 | 示例 |
|------|----------|----------|------|
| 一般现在时 | do/does | am/is/are + done | English **is spoken** worldwide. |
| 一般过去时 | did | was/were + done | The bridge **was built** in 1990. |
| 一般将来时 | will do | will be + done | The work **will be finished** tomorrow. |
| 现在进行时 | am/is/are doing | am/is/are being + done | The car **is being repaired** now. |
| 过去进行时 | was/were doing | was/were being + done | The house **was being painted** then. |
| 现在完成时 | have/has done | have/has been + done | The task **has been completed**. |
| 过去完成时 | had done | had been + done | The letter **had been sent** before I arrived. |
| 将来完成时 | will have done | will have been + done | The project **will have been finished** by next month. |

## 三、特殊被动语态结构

### 3.1 双宾语动词的被动

双宾语动词（give, tell, show, teach, offer, send）可以将**间接宾语**或**直接宾语**变为被动句的主语。

**例句**：
1. He gave **me** a book. → **I** was given a book. （简单 - 间接宾语做主语）
2. He gave me **a book**. → **A book** was given to me. （简单 - 直接宾语做主语）
3. The professor **was offered** a prestigious award by the international academic committee for her groundbreaking contributions to the field of quantum physics. （复杂）

### 3.2 含宾补的被动

**例句**：
1. They call him Tom. → He **is called** Tom. （简单）
2. They elected her president. → She **was elected** president. （中等）
3. The committee **was made** to reconsider its original decision after new evidence **was presented** that **was considered** highly relevant to the case. （复杂）

### 3.3 情态动词的被动

**构成**：情态动词 + be + done

**例句**：
1. The work **must be finished** today. （简单）
2. This problem **can be solved** in several different ways. （中等）
3. The environmental impact assessment **should have been conducted** before the construction project **was approved**, and all potential risks **ought to be evaluated** more carefully in the future. （复杂 - should have been done 表示"本应该被做"）

## 四、不能用被动语态的情况

| 情况 | 示例 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| 不及物动词 | happen, occur, take place | ~~It was happened.~~ → It **happened**. |
| 系动词 | be, look, seem, feel | ~~She is looked happy.~~ → She **looks** happy. |
| 表示状态的及物动词 | have（拥有）, cost, fit | ~~The book is had by me.~~ → I **have** the book. |
| 反身代词做宾语 | She dressed herself. | 不变被动 |

> [!warning] 易错提醒
> - **happen/occur/take place** 是不及物动词，不能用被动：
>   - ~~The accident was happened.~~ → The accident **happened**.
> - **consist of** 无被动：~~Water is consisted of H and O.~~ → Water **consists of** H and O.
> - **belong to** 无被动：~~The book is belonged to me.~~ → The book **belongs to** me.
> - **be seated / be located / be situated** 本身就是被动形式，不要再加 be：
>   - Please **be seated**.（请坐）

## 五、被动语态的使用场景

> [!tip] 实战技巧
> **什么时候用被动语态？**
> 1. **不知道谁做的**：My bike **was stolen** last night.（不知道谁偷的）
> 2. **不重要谁做的**：The Great Wall **was built** over 2,000 years ago.（强调长城，而非建造者）
> 3. **学术/正式写作**：The data **were collected** through surveys.（客观、正式）
> 4. **强调承受者**：The patient **was treated** immediately.（关注点在病人）
>
> **阅读中**：学术论文大量使用被动语态，识别 be + done 结构后，重点关注"什么被做了"。
>
> **写作中**：
> - 学术写作中适当使用被动，避免过多 "I/We"
> - 日常写作中主动语态更生动有力
> - 避免过度使用被动导致句子冗长
