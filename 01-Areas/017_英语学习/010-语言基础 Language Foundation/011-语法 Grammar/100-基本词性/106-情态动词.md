# 情态动词

**核心概括**：情态动词表示说话人对动作的态度（能力、许可、义务、推测等），后面必须接**动词原形**，不能单独作谓语，没有人称和数的变化。

> [!info] 语法规则
> - 情态动词 + **动词原形**（不加 to，ought to 除外）
> - 情态动词没有第三人称单数变化（不加 -s）
> - 否定形式：情态动词 + not
> - 疑问形式：情态动词提前到主语前

---

## 一、常见情态动词及其用法

### 1.1 can / could — 能力与许可

| 用法 | can | could |
|------|-----|-------|
| 表示能力 | I **can** swim.（我会游泳） | I **could** swim when I was five.（我五岁时就会游泳） |
| 表示许可 | **Can** I use your phone?（较随意） | **Could** I use your phone?（更礼貌） |
| 表示可能性 | It **can** be dangerous.（可能有危险） | It **could** rain later.（可能会下雨） |

**例句**：
1. She **can** speak three languages fluently. （简单 - 能力）
2. **Could** you please tell me how to get to the nearest subway station? （中等 - 礼貌请求）
3. The new technology **could** potentially revolutionize the way we communicate, although experts argue that it **cannot** completely replace face-to-face interaction. （复杂 - 可能性 + 否定）

### 1.2 may / might — 许可与推测

| 用法 | may | might |
|------|-----|-------|
| 表示许可 | **May** I come in?（正式许可） | — |
| 表示推测 | He **may** be at home.（可能在家，50%） | He **might** be at home.（也许在家，30%） |

**例句**：
1. **May** I ask you a question? （简单 - 正式许可）
2. She **might** come to the party if she finishes her work early. （中等 - 较低可能性）
3. The results **may** not **be** entirely accurate due to the limited sample size, and the conclusions drawn from this study **might** need to **be** reconsidered in light of more recent evidence. （复杂 - 学术语境中的谨慎表达）

### 1.3 must — 必须与推测

| 用法 | 肯定 | 否定 |
|------|------|------|
| 义务/必须 | You **must** finish it today. | You **mustn't** smoke here.（禁止） |
| 推测/一定 | He **must** be tired.（一定很累，90%+） | — （否定推测不用 mustn't，用 can't） |

**例句**：
1. You **must** wear a seatbelt. （简单 - 义务）
2. He **must** be working late — his office light is still on. （中等 - 肯定推测）
3. Given the overwhelming evidence and the consistency of the experimental results, the researchers concluded that there **must** be a previously undiscovered mechanism at work, which **must** not be overlooked in future investigations. （复杂 - 强烈推测）

> [!warning] 易错提醒
> - **must** 的否定推测不是 mustn't，而是 **can't**：
>   - He **must** be at home.（他一定在家）
>   - He **can't** be at home.（他不可能在家）
>   - ~~He mustn't be at home.~~（mustn't 只表示"禁止"）
> - **must** 没有过去式，过去的"必须"用 **had to**

### 1.4 should / ought to — 应该

**例句**：
1. You **should** study harder. （简单 - 建议）
2. Students **ought to** respect their teachers and follow the school rules. （中等 - ought to 更正式）
3. The government **should** have implemented stricter regulations before the crisis **occurred**; they **ought to** have anticipated the risks and taken preventive measures. （复杂 - should/ought to have done 表示"本应该做但没做"）

### 1.5 need — 需要

| 用法 | 作情态动词 | 作实义动词 |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| 肯定 | — （一般不用） | I **need to** go now. |
| 否定 | You **needn't** worry. | You **don't need to** worry. |
| 疑问 | **Need** I go? | **Do** I **need to** go? |

**例句**：
1. You **needn't** bring anything. （简单 - 不必要）
2. She **doesn't need to** worry about the exam because she has prepared thoroughly. （中等 - 实义动词用法）
3. The participants **need not** have completed the entire questionnaire, as the researchers only **needed to** analyze the first three sections for the preliminary findings. （复杂 - 情态动词 + 实义动词两种用法并存）

## 二、情态动词表推测的概率排序

> [!info] 语法规则
> 从高到低的确信程度：

| 情态动词 | 概率 | 示例 |
|----------|------|------|
| must | ~95% | He **must** be a doctor. （他一定是医生） |
| will/would | ~90% | That **will** be John at the door. （那一定是约翰） |
| should/ought to | ~80% | She **should** be there by now. （她应该到了） |
| can | ~50% | It **can** get very cold here. （这里可能很冷） |
| may | ~50% | He **may** come tomorrow. （他可能明天来） |
| could | ~30% | It **could** be true. （也许是真的） |
| might | ~25% | She **might** change her mind. （她也许会改主意） |

## 三、情态动词 + have done（对过去的推测/虚拟）

> [!info] 语法规则
> **情态动词 + have + 过去分词**：对过去已发生事件的推测或未实现的虚拟

| 结构 | 含义 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| must have done | 过去一定做了 | She **must have forgotten** the meeting. |
| can't/couldn't have done | 过去不可能做了 | He **can't have passed** the exam. |
| may/might have done | 过去可能做了 | They **may have left** already. |
| should have done | 过去应该做（但没做） | You **should have told** me earlier. |
| needn't have done | 过去没必要做（但做了） | You **needn't have bought** so much food. |

**例句**：
1. He **must have taken** the wrong bus. （简单 - 对过去的推测）
2. You **should have studied** harder for the exam; now you have to take it again. （中等 - 责备/遗憾）
3. The investigators concluded that the suspect **could not have committed** the crime at the time stated by the prosecution, as multiple witnesses confirmed that he **must have been** at a different location, and the evidence **might have been** tampered with. （复杂 - 多种对过去的推测）

> [!warning] 易错提醒
> - **should have done** ≠ should do：前者表示"过去应该做但没做"（带遗憾/责备语气）
> - **needn't have done** ≠ didn't need to do：
>   - needn't have done：做了，但其实没必要做
>   - didn't need to do：没必要做，所以没做
> - **could have done** 有两个含义：1）过去可能做了（推测）；2）过去本能做但没做（虚拟）

> [!tip] 实战技巧
> **阅读中**：
> - 在学术论文中，may/might/could 常用于表达谨慎的结论，表示作者不完全确定
> - must 表示作者非常确信的推断
>
> **写作中**：
> - 学术写作中用 may/might/could 表达不确定的结论，比 must 更谨慎
> - 用 should have done 表达遗憾和反思，适合反思性写作
> - 口语和非正式写作中可用 can 代替 may 表示许可
