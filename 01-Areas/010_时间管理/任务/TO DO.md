## 任务详情
```dataview
TABLE
    text AS "任务描述",
    due AS "截止日期",
    (date(due) - date(now)).days AS "剩余天数"
FROM "01-Areas/010_时间管理/GTD管理模板工具/004_执行清单"
WHERE !completed
SORT due DESC
```
