(1)给出 (a, (a, a)) 和 (((a, a), Λ , (a)), a) 的最左和最右推导。

字符串 1： (a, (a, a))

最左推导：

$$\begin{aligned} S &\Rightarrow (T) \\ &\Rightarrow (T, S) \\ &\Rightarrow (S, S) \quad &(T \to S) \\ &\Rightarrow (a, S) \quad &(S \to a) \\ &\Rightarrow (a, (T)) \quad &(S \to (T)) \\ &\Rightarrow (a, (T, S)) \quad &(T \to T, S) \\ &\Rightarrow (a, (S, S)) \quad &(T \to S) \\ &\Rightarrow (a, (a, S)) \quad &(S \to a) \\ &\Rightarrow (a, (a, a)) \quad &(S \to a) \end{aligned}$$


最右推导:  

$$\begin{aligned} S &\Rightarrow (T) \\ &\Rightarrow (T, S) \\ &\Rightarrow (T, (T)) \quad &(S \to (T)) \\ &\Rightarrow (T, (T, S)) \quad &(T \to T, S) \\ &\Rightarrow (T, (T, a)) \quad &(S \to a) \\ &\Rightarrow (T, (S, a)) \quad &(T \to S) \\ &\Rightarrow (T, (a, a)) \quad &(S \to a) \\ &\Rightarrow (S, (a, a)) \quad &(T \to S) \\ &\Rightarrow (a, (a, a)) \quad &(S \to a) \end{aligned}$$
字符串2： (((a, a), Λ, (a)), a) 的推导

最左推导：

$$\begin{aligned} S &\Rightarrow (T) \\ &\Rightarrow (T, S) \quad &(T \to T, S) \\ &\Rightarrow (T, a) \quad &(S \to a) \\ &\Rightarrow (S, a) \quad &(T \to S) \\ &\Rightarrow ((T), a) \quad &(S \to (T)) \\ &\Rightarrow ((T, S), a) \quad &(T \to T, S) \\ &\Rightarrow ((T, (T)), a) \quad &(S \to (T)) \\ &\Rightarrow ((T, (S)), a) \quad &(T \to S) \\ &\Rightarrow ((T, (a)), a) \quad &(S \to a) \\ &\Rightarrow ((T, S), a) \quad &(S \to (T)) \\ &\Rightarrow (S, a) \quad &(T \to S) \\ &\Rightarrow (((T), S), a) \quad &(T \to S \to (T)) \\ &\Rightarrow (((T, S), S), a) \quad &(T \to T, S) \\ &\Rightarrow (((T, S), \Lambda), a) \quad &(S \to \Lambda) \\ &\Rightarrow (((T, S), \Lambda), a) \quad \\ &\Rightarrow (((T, (T)), \Lambda), a) \quad &(S \to (T)) \\ &\Rightarrow (((T, (S)), \Lambda), a) \quad &(T \to S) \\ &\Rightarrow (((T, (a)), \Lambda), a) \quad &(S \to a) \\ &\Rightarrow (((T, S), \Lambda), a) \quad &(S \to (T)) \\ &\Rightarrow (((S, S), \Lambda), a) \quad &(T \to S) \\ &\Rightarrow (((a, S), \Lambda), a) \quad &(S \to a) \\ &\Rightarrow (((a, a), \Lambda), a) \quad &(S \to a) \end{aligned}$$

最右推导：  

$$\begin{aligned} S &\Rightarrow (T) \\ &\Rightarrow (T, S) \\ &\Rightarrow (T, a) \quad &(S \to a) \\ &\Rightarrow (T, S) \quad &(S \to a) \\ &\Rightarrow ((T), S) \quad &(T \to S \to (T)) \\ &\Rightarrow ((T, S), S) \quad &(T \to T, S) \\ &\Rightarrow ((T, (T)), S) \quad &(S \to (T)) \\ &\Rightarrow ((T, (S)), S) \quad &(T \to S) \\ &\Rightarrow ((T, (a)), S) \quad &(S \to a) \\ &\Rightarrow ((T, S), S) \quad &(S \to (T)) \\ &\Rightarrow ((T, S), a) \quad &(S \to a) \\ &\Rightarrow ((T, S), a) \\ &\Rightarrow (((T), S), a) \\ &\Rightarrow (((T, S), S), a) \\ &\Rightarrow (((T, (T)), S), a) \\ &\Rightarrow (((T, (S)), S), a) \\ &\Rightarrow (((T, (a)), S), a) \\ &\Rightarrow (((T, S), S), a) \\ &\Rightarrow (((S, S), S), a) \\ &\Rightarrow (((a, S), S), a) \\ &\Rightarrow (((a, a), S), a) \\ &\Rightarrow (((a, a), \Lambda), a) \\ &\Rightarrow (((a, a), \Lambda, (a)), a) \end{aligned}$$
