# 非谓语动词

**核心概括**：非谓语动词是指在句中不充当谓语的动词形式，包括不定式（to do）、动名词（doing）、现在分词（doing）和过去分词（done），它们可以在句中充当主语、宾语、定语、状语和补语。

> [!info] 语法规则
> - **不定式 to do**：表示目的、将来、主动
> - **动名词 doing**：表示一般性动作，做主语/宾语（名词性质）
> - **现在分词 doing**：表示主动、正在进行（形容词/副词性质）
> - **过去分词 done**：表示被动、已完成（形容词/副词性质）
> - 一个简单句中只能有**一个谓语动词**，其余动词必须变为非谓语形式

---

## 一、动词不定式（to do）

### 1.1 做主语

**例句**：
1. **To learn** English is important. （简单）
2. **To master** a foreign language requires years of consistent practice and dedication. （中等）
3. **To have been selected** for this prestigious program is considered a great honor by students and faculty alike. （复杂 - 完成式不定式做主语）

> [!tip] 实战技巧
> 不定式做主语时，常用 **it** 做形式主语，把真正的不定式主语后移：
> - ~~To learn English is important.~~ → **It** is important **to learn** English.

### 1.2 做宾语

只接不定式做宾语的动词：want, wish, hope, decide, plan, agree, refuse, promise, manage, afford, pretend, offer

**例句**：
1. I **want to go** home. （简单）
2. She **decided to accept** the job offer after careful consideration. （中等）
3. The company **agreed to invest** a significant amount of capital in the research project, and the team **managed to complete** the prototype ahead of schedule, which **enabled** them **to secure** additional funding. （复杂）

### 1.3 做目的状语

**例句**：
1. He went to the library **to borrow** some books. （简单）
2. She worked overtime every day **to save** enough money for her trip abroad. （中等）
3. **In order to** achieve sustainable development, the government has implemented a series of policies **designed to** reduce carbon emissions and **to promote** the use of renewable energy sources. （复杂 - in order to 更正式）

### 1.4 做定语（后置）

**例句**：
1. I have a lot of work **to do**. （简单）
2. She was the first person **to arrive** at the meeting and the last one **to leave**. （中等）
3. The committee established a comprehensive framework **to evaluate** the effectiveness of educational programs, providing teachers with practical tools **to implement** in their classrooms. （复杂）

## 二、动名词（doing）

### 2.1 做主语

**例句**：
1. **Swimming** is good exercise. （简单）
2. **Reading** books regularly helps improve vocabulary and critical thinking skills. （中等）
3. **Having been exposed** to diverse cultures during his travels **broadened** his perspective and **fundamentally changed** his understanding of the world. （复杂 - 完成式动名词做主语）

### 2.2 做宾语

只接动名词做宾语的动词：enjoy, finish, mind, avoid, suggest, practice, consider, imagine, keep, deny, risk, admit, appreciate

**例句**：
1. I **enjoy reading** novels. （简单）
2. She **couldn't help laughing** when she heard the joke. （中等）
3. The authorities **denied having received** any complaints and **avoided answering** questions about the allegations, which led many people to **consider filing** a formal investigation request. （复杂）

### 2.3 接不定式 vs. 动名词含义不同的动词

| 动词 | + to do | + doing |
|------|---------|---------|
| remember | 记得去做（未做） | 记得做过（已做） |
| forget | 忘记去做（未做） | 忘记做过（已做） |
| stop | 停下来去做（另一件事） | 停止做（正在做的事） |
| try | 尝试做（努力） | 试着做（实验性） |
| mean | 打算做 | 意味着 |
| regret | 遗憾地说（将要做的事） | 后悔做过（已做的事） |
| go on | 接着做（另一件事） | 继续做（同一件事） |

**例句**：
1. I remember **locking** the door. （我记得锁了门 - 已做）
   I must remember **to lock** the door. （我得记得去锁门 - 未做）
2. She stopped **talking** when the teacher came in. （她停止说话 - 停止正在做的事）
   She stopped **to talk** to her friend. （她停下来和朋友聊天 - 停下来去做另一件事）
3. Having a high GDP **means** having a strong economy, but it doesn't **mean to** suggest that all citizens benefit equally from economic growth. （复杂 - means doing 意味着 vs. mean to do 打算）

> [!warning] 易错提醒
> - **suggest/recommend/advise** 后接 **doing**（不接 to do）：
>   - He suggested **going** to the park.（不是 ~~suggested to go~~）
> - **enjoy/finish/mind** 后只能接 **doing**：
>   - I enjoy **playing** tennis.（不是 ~~enjoy to play~~）

## 三、现在分词（doing）与过去分词（done）

### 3.1 核心区别

| | 现在分词 doing | 过去分词 done |
|------|--------------|--------------|
| 语态 | **主动** | **被动** |
| 时间 | 正在进行 | 已经完成 |
| 关系 | 名词发出动作 | 名词承受动作 |
| 感受 | 令人...的（事物） | 感到...的（人） |

**例句对比**：
- The movie is **boring**. （电影令人无聊 - 电影发出"无聊"这个效果）
- I am **bored**. （我感到无聊 - 我承受"无聊"这个感觉）

### 3.2 做定语

**例句**：
1. the **sleeping** baby（正在睡觉的婴儿）/ the **broken** window（被打碎的窗户）（简单）
2. The man **standing** at the door is my father. / The book **written** by this author is popular. （中等 - 后置定语）
3. The data **collected** from the participants **participating** in the study **revealed** a significant correlation between the **measured** variables and the **predicted** outcomes. （复杂 - 多个分词做定语）

### 3.3 做状语

**例句**：
1. **Hearing** the news, she burst into tears. （简单 - 时间状语，主动）
2. **Encouraged** by the positive feedback, he decided to continue his research. （中等 - 原因状语，被动）
3. **Having completed** the preliminary analysis and **having been informed** of the new requirements, the research team proceeded to redesign the experimental framework, **taking** into account all the previously **overlooked** factors. （复杂 - 完成式 + 被动式 + 伴随状语）

> [!warning] 易错提醒
> 分词做状语时，其逻辑主语必须与句子主语一致（否则构成"悬垂分词"错误）：
> - ~~Walking down the street, the building caught my eye.~~（建筑不能走路）
> - **Walking** down the street, **I** noticed a beautiful building.（正确 - I 是 walking 的主语）

> [!tip] 实战技巧
> **阅读中**：
> - 遇到 -ing/-ed 形式，先判断是做谓语还是非谓语
> - 非谓语的逻辑主语就是句子主语（做状语时）或被修饰的名词（做定语时）
>
> **写作中**：
> - 用分词做状语可以使句子更简洁，替代状语从句
> - Because he was tired → **Being** tired / **Tired**, he went to bed early.
> - After she finished → **Having finished** the work, she went home.
