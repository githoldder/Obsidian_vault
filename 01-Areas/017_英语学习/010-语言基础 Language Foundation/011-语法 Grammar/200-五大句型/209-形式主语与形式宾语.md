# 形式主语与形式宾语

**核心概括**：英语中用 **it** 做形式主语或形式宾语，将真正的主语（不定式/从句）或宾语后移，避免句子头重脚轻，使结构更平衡。

> [!info] 语法规则
> - **形式主语**：**It** + be/动词 + ... + 真正主语（to do / that从句）
> - **形式宾语**：主语 + 动词 + **it** + 补语 + 真正宾语（to do / that从句）
> - it 本身没有实际意义，只是占位符
> - 真正的主语/宾语在句子后面

---

## 一、形式主语

### 1.1 It is + adj. + to do（做某事是...的）

最常见的形式主语句型，it 代替不定式做主语。

**例句**：
1. **It** is important **to learn** English. （简单）
2. **It** is necessary **to take** immediate action to address the growing environmental crisis. （中等）
3. **It** would be extremely difficult, if not entirely impossible, **to accurately predict** the long-term consequences of implementing such a radical policy change without first conducting a thorough analysis of all the variables involved. （复杂）

> [!info] 语法规则
> 常见搭配形容词：important, necessary, difficult, easy, possible, impossible, dangerous, wise, foolish, kind, polite, rude, wrong

### 1.2 It is + adj. + that...（...是...的）

it 代替 that 从句做主语。

**例句**：
1. **It** is obvious **that** he is lying. （简单）
2. **It** is widely acknowledged **that** education plays a vital role in social development. （中等）
3. **It** has become increasingly apparent **that** the traditional approaches to urban planning are no longer adequate for addressing the complex challenges posed by rapid population growth, climate change, and the digital transformation of modern economies. （复杂）

常见句型：

| 结构 | 含义 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| It is clear/obvious that... | 很明显... | **It** is clear **that** she is talented. |
| It is likely/unlikely that... | 很可能/不太可能... | **It** is likely **that** it will rain. |
| It is important/necessary that... | ...很重要/必要 | **It** is important **that** we act now. |
| It is strange/surprising that... | 奇怪/令人惊讶的是... | **It** is surprising **that** he won. |

### 1.3 It is + done + that...（据.../人们...）

it 代替 that 从句做主语，主句用**被动语态**。

| 结构 | 含义 |
|------|------|
| It is said that... | 据说... |
| It is reported that... | 据报道... |
| It is believed that... | 人们认为... |
| It is estimated that... | 据估计... |
| It is suggested that... | 有人建议... |
| It is well known that... | 众所周知... |
| It is thought that... | 人们认为... |
| It is expected that... | 预计... |

**例句**：
1. **It** is said **that** he is a genius. （简单）
2. **It** is widely believed **that** regular exercise can significantly reduce the risk of chronic diseases. （中等）
3. **It** has been repeatedly demonstrated in peer-reviewed studies **that** early childhood intervention programs not only improve academic outcomes but also reduce criminal behavior in adulthood, and **it** is now estimated **that** every dollar invested in such programs yields approximately seven dollars in long-term social benefits. （复杂 - 两个 it 形式主语句）

> [!tip] 实战技巧
> "It is said that..." 可以转换为 "sb. is said to do..."：
> - **It** is said **that** he is a genius. = **He** is said **to be** a genius.
> - **It** is reported **that** the company made a profit. = **The company** is reported **to have made** a profit.

## 二、形式宾语

### 2.1 make / find / think / consider + it + adj. + to do

动词后用 it 做形式宾语，真正的宾语（to do / that从句）后移。

**例句**：
1. I find **it** difficult **to learn** French. （简单 - find it adj to do）
2. Technology has made **it** possible **to communicate** with people around the world instantly. （中等 - make it adj to do）
3. The committee considered **it** necessary **to conduct** a thorough review of all existing safety protocols before authorizing the construction of the new facility, and they made **it** clear **that** no exceptions would be permitted regardless of the cost implications. （复杂 - consider it adj to do + make it adj that）

### 2.2 make / find / think + it + adj. + that...

**例句**：
1. I think **it** important **that** we should arrive on time. （简单）
2. She made **it** clear **that** she would not tolerate any further delays. （中等）
3. The researchers found **it** remarkable **that** such a simple intervention could produce **such** profound and lasting changes in participants' behavior, and they considered **it** essential **that** the experiment be replicated across different cultural contexts before any definitive conclusions were drawn. （复杂）

### 2.3 常见搭配动词

| 动词 | 结构 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| make | make it + adj. + to do/that | make it possible to... |
| find | find it + adj. + to do/that | find it hard to... |
| think | think it + adj./n. + to do/that | think it necessary to... |
| consider | consider it + adj. + to do/that | consider it important to... |
| feel | feel it + adj. + to do/that | feel it necessary to... |
| regard | regard it as + adj./n. + to do | regard it as a duty to... |

## 三、it 做形式主语/宾语 vs. 其他 it 用法

> [!warning] 易错提醒
> it 在英语中有多种用法，注意区分：
>
> | 用法 | 示例 | 说明 |
> |------|------|------|
> | 人称代词 | **It** is a good book. I like **it**. | 指代具体事物 |
> | 形式主语 | **It** is important to study. | it 代替 to study |
> | 形式宾语 | I find **it** hard to believe. | it 代替 to believe |
> | 强调句 | **It** is Tom **that** did it. | 强调 Tom |
> | 天气/时间/距离 | **It** is raining. / **It** is 5 o'clock. | 无实义 |
>
> **判断形式主语 vs. 强调句的方法**：
> - 去掉 It is/was 和 that，如果剩下的部分是**完整句子** → **强调句**
> - 如果不完整 → **形式主语**
>
> 例：
> - **It** is important **that** we study hard. → 去掉后：important we study hard（不完整）→ 形式主语
> - **It** is Tom **that** studies hard. → 去掉后：Tom studies hard（完整）→ 强调句

> [!tip] 实战技巧
> **阅读中**：
> - 遇到句首的 It is...that，先判断是形式主语还是强调句
> - 形式主语句的重点信息在 that 从句或 to do 结构中
>
> **写作中**：
> - It is + adj. + to do 是议论文的经典开头：It is essential to recognize that...
> - It is + done + that 适合引用观点/研究发现：It is well documented that...
> - make it clear/possible/easy that/to do 是论证中的高频表达
