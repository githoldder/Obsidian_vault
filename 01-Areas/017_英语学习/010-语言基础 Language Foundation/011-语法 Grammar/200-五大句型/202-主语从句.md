# 主语从句

**核心概括**：主语从句是用一个完整的句子充当另一个句子的主语，由连接词（that/what/how/where/when/why/whether/who）引导，在句中起到名词的作用。

> [!info] 语法规则
> - 主语从句在句中做**主语**，谓语动词通常用**第三人称单数**
> - 引导词不能省略（区别于宾语从句中 that 可省略）
> - 为避免头重脚轻，常用 **it** 做形式主语，把主语从句后移

---

## 一、that 引导的主语从句

that 在从句中**不充当成分**，只起连接作用，但**不能省略**。

**例句**：
1. **That he is honest** is well known. （简单）
2. **That she could complete the project on time** surprised everyone in the office. （中等）
3. **That the government decided to invest heavily in renewable energy despite strong opposition from traditional industries** demonstrates a clear commitment to long-term environmental sustainability. （复杂 - 长主语从句）

### 用 it 做形式主语（更常用）

> [!info] 语法规则
> 常见句型：
> - **It is + adj. + that...** （It is obvious that...）
> - **It is + n. + that...** （It is a fact that...）
> - **It is + done + that...** （It is reported that...）

**例句**：
1. **It** is clear **that** he has made a mistake. （简单）
2. **It** is widely believed **that** education plays a crucial role in social mobility. （中等）
3. **It** has been scientifically demonstrated **that** prolonged exposure to high levels of air pollution significantly increases the risk of developing respiratory diseases, particularly **among** vulnerable populations such as children and the elderly. （复杂 - it is done that...结构）

常见的 "It is + done + that" 结构：

| 结构 | 含义 |
|------|------|
| It is said that... | 据说... |
| It is reported that... | 据报道... |
| It is believed that... | 人们认为... |
| It is estimated that... | 据估计... |
| It is suggested that... | 有人建议... |
| It is well known that... | 众所周知... |

## 二、what 引导的主语从句

what = the thing(s) that，在从句中充当**主语、宾语或表语**。

**例句**：
1. **What he said** is true. （简单 - what 做从句中的宾语）
2. **What matters most** is not how much you earn but how you spend your time. （中等 - what 做从句中的主语）
3. **What the researchers discovered during the five-year longitudinal study** fundamentally challenged the assumptions that had dominated the field of developmental psychology for over half a century. （复杂）

> [!warning] 易错提醒
> **what** vs. **that** 引导主语从句的区别：
> - **that** 在从句中不做成分，只是连接词
> - **what** 在从句中必须做成分（主语/宾语/表语）
> - **That** he came surprised me.（that 不做成分，从句完整）
> - **What** he said surprised me.（what 做 said 的宾语，从句缺宾语）

## 三、其他连接词引导的主语从句

### 3.1 how 引导

**例句**：
1. **How he solved the problem** remains a mystery. （简单）
2. **How we educate the next generation** will determine the future of our society. （中等）
3. **How effectively a country manages the transition from traditional manufacturing to a knowledge-based economy** largely depends on the quality of its educational infrastructure and the flexibility of its labor market policies. （复杂）

### 3.2 where 引导

**例句**：
1. **Where he lives** is unknown. （简单）
2. **Where the missing plane crashed** has not been determined yet. （中等）
3. **Where the boundary lies between legitimate government surveillance and the violation of individual privacy rights** has become one of the most contentious legal and ethical questions of the digital age. （复杂）

### 3.3 when 引导

**例句**：
1. **When he will arrive** is uncertain. （简单）
2. **When the next economic recession will occur** is difficult to predict. （中等）
3. **When the full impact of artificial intelligence on the global labor market will become apparent** is a question that economists, policymakers, and technology experts continue to debate without reaching a consensus. （复杂）

### 3.4 whether 引导

> [!warning] 易错提醒
> 主语从句中只能用 **whether**，**不能用 if**：
> - **Whether** he will come is still unknown. （正确）
> - ~~**If** he will come is still unknown.~~ （错误 - if 不能引导主语从句）

**例句**：
1. **Whether he agrees** doesn't matter. （简单）
2. **Whether the new policy will achieve its intended goals** remains to be seen. （中等）
3. **Whether the benefits of genetic engineering outweigh the potential risks to biodiversity and human health** is a question that the scientific community, regulatory agencies, and the general public must carefully consider before widespread implementation is authorized. （复杂）

### 3.5 why / who 引导

**例句**：
1. **Why he left** is unclear. / **Who did it** is unknown. （简单）
2. **Why she suddenly changed her mind** puzzled everyone at the meeting. （中等）
3. **Who will ultimately bear the responsibility for the environmental damage caused by decades of industrial pollution** is a question that has profound legal, moral, and financial implications for both corporations and governments. （复杂）

## 四、主语从句的语序

> [!warning] 易错提醒
> 主语从句必须使用**陈述语序**（主语 + 谓语），不能用疑问语序：
> - **What he needs** is rest.（正确 - 陈述语序）
> - ~~What does he need is rest.~~（错误 - 疑问语序）
> - **When he will come** is uncertain.（正确）
> - ~~When will he come is uncertain.~~（错误）

> [!tip] 实战技巧
> **阅读中**：
> - 遇到 It is/was... that 结构，先判断是主语从句的形式主语还是强调句
> - 判断方法：去掉 It is/was 和 that，如果剩下的部分是一个完整句子 → 强调句；如果不完整 → 形式主语
>
> **写作中**：
> - 写议论文时，用 "It is widely acknowledged that..." 开头引出观点
> - 用 "What is noteworthy is that..." 引出值得注意的点
> - 避免头重脚轻：长主语从句建议用 it 做形式主语后移
