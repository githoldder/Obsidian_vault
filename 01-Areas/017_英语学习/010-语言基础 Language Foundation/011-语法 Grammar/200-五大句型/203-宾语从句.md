# 宾语从句

**核心概括**：宾语从句是用一个完整的句子充当动词或介词的宾语，由 that、连接代词/副词或 whether/if 引导，是最常见的名词性从句。

> [!info] 语法规则
> - 宾语从句在句中做**动词的宾语**或**介词的宾语**
> - **that** 引导的宾语从句中，that 可以省略（口语中常省略）
> - 从句必须使用**陈述语序**
> - 主句为过去时，从句一般要**时态后移**（时态一致原则）

---

## 一、that 引导的宾语从句

that 在从句中不做成分，只起连接作用，常可**省略**。

**例句**：
1. I think **(that)** he is right. （简单 - that 可省略）
2. She told me **(that)** she would be late because of the heavy traffic. （中等 - 时态后移）
3. The study concluded **(that)** exposure to natural environments significantly reduces stress levels, **(that)** the benefits are more pronounced in urban populations, and **(that)** even brief periods of contact with nature can produce measurable improvements in mental well-being. （复杂 - 多个 that 并列宾语从句，后两个 that 不可省略）

> [!warning] 易错提醒
> **that 不能省略的情况**：
> - 多个宾语从句并列时，第二个及之后的 that 不能省略
> - 宾语从句前有插入语时：I believe, **that** without doubt, he will succeed.
> - 形式宾语 it + 宾语从句：I find **it** important **that**...

---

## 二、连接代词/副词引导的宾语从句

| 引导词 | 在从句中做 | 示例 |
|--------|-----------|------|
| what | 主语/宾语/表语 | I don't know **what** he wants. |
| who/whom | 主语/宾语 | Tell me **who** did it. |
| which | 主语/宾语 | I wonder **which** one is better. |
| how | 方式状语 | Show me **how** you did it. |
| where | 地点状语 | I don't know **where** he lives. |
| when | 时间状语 | Tell me **when** you will come. |
| why | 原因状语 | I wonder **why** she left. |

**例句**：
1. I don't know **what** he wants. （简单 - what 做宾语）
2. Can you tell me **where** the nearest bank is and **how** I can get there? （中等 - where/how 引导并列宾语从句）
3. The investigation sought to determine **who** had been responsible for the data breach, **how** the unauthorized access had occurred, **when** the security vulnerability had first appeared, and **why** the IT department had failed to detect the intrusion for such an extended period. （复杂 - 四个宾语从句并列）

---

## 三、whether / if 引导的宾语从句

表示"是否"。

| | whether | if |
|---|---------|-----|
| 引导宾语从句 | 可以 | 可以 |
| 后接 or not | **whether** or not（紧跟） | if... or not（分开） |
| 介词后 | 只能用 **whether** | 不能用 if |
| 引导主语从句 | 只能用 **whether** | 不能用 if |

**例句**：
1. I don't know **whether/if** he will come. （简单）
2. She asked me **whether** I was interested in the position **or not**. （中等 - whether...or not）
3. The committee is still debating **whether** the proposed changes to the curriculum will adequately prepare students for the challenges they will face in an increasingly automated workforce, and several members have expressed uncertainty about **whether** the benefits outweigh the potential disruptions to the existing educational framework. （复杂）

---

## 四、介词后的宾语从句

介词后面可以接由 what/how/whether 等引导的宾语从句。

> [!warning] 易错提醒
> 介词后只能用 **whether**，不能用 if：
> - It depends on **whether** he agrees.（正确）
> - ~~It depends on **if** he agrees.~~（错误）
> 介词后不能直接用 **that** 引导宾语从句（except/in that 除外）

**例句**：
1. It depends on **what** you want. （简单）
2. I am not sure about **whether** this approach will work in practice. （中等）
3. The success of the initiative largely depends on **how** effectively the local government collaborates with community organizations, and the outcome will vary according to **what** resources are available in each region and **whether** the stakeholders demonstrate a genuine commitment to the project's goals. （复杂）

---

## 五、时态一致原则

> [!info] 语法规则
> 当主句为**过去时态**时，宾语从句的时态通常需要相应后移：
>
> | 主句时态 | 从句时态变化 |
> |----------|-------------|
> | 一般过去时 | 一般现在时 → 一般过去时 |
> | 一般过去时 | 现在进行时 → 过去进行时 |
> | 一般过去时 | 现在完成时 → 过去完成时 |
> | 一般过去时 | 一般将来时 → 过去将来时 (would) |

**例外**：从句表示**客观事实、科学真理、谚语**时，**不需要时态后移**，仍用一般现在时。

**例句**：
1. He **said** (that) he **was** tired. （简单 - is → was）
2. She **told** me (that) she **had finished** the report and **would submit** it the next day. （中等 - has finished → had finished, will → would）
3. The teacher **explained** that the Earth **revolves** around the Sun and that gravity **is** a fundamental force of nature, but he **added** that the students **should have studied** these concepts before the exam **had begun**. （复杂 - 客观真理不变时态 + 正常时态后移）

> [!warning] 易错提醒
> - 客观真理永远用**一般现在时**：
>   - He told me that the earth **is**（不是 was）round.
> - 从句中有明确的过去时间状语时，用**一般过去时**，不用过去完成时：
>   - He said he **visited**（不是 had visited）Paris **in 2020**.

> [!tip] 实战技巧
> **阅读中**：
> - 看到动词后面跟着 that/what/how/whether，立即识别为宾语从句
> - 复杂句中多个宾语从句并列，用连接词帮助理清每个从句的边界
>
> **写作中**：
> - 转述他人观点时善用宾语从句：The author argues that... / Studies suggest that...
> - 学术写作中，引用研究发现常用：The data indicate that... / The results show that...
> - 表达不确定性用 whether：It remains unclear whether...
