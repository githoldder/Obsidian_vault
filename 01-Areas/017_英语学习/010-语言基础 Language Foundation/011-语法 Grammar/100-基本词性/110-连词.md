# 连词

**核心概括**：连词是连接词与词、短语与短语、句子与句子的纽带，分为并列连词（连接对等成分）和从属连词（引导从句），是构建复杂句子的关键工具。

> [!info] 语法规则
> - **并列连词**：连接两个**语法地位对等**的成分（词+词、句+句）
> - **从属连词**：引导**从句**（主语从句、宾语从句、状语从句等），使从句依附于主句
> - 并列连词前后的成分必须**对称**（名词对名词，动词对动词）
> - 从属连词引导的从句不能独立成句

## 一、并列连词

### 1.1 and — 表示并列、递进

**例句**：
1. Tom **and** Jerry are good friends. （简单 - 连接两个名词）
2. She finished her homework **and** went to bed early. （中等 - 连接两个动作）
3. The new policy aims to promote economic growth **and** to reduce income inequality, **and** it has been widely supported by both the business community **and** social welfare organizations. （复杂 - 多层并列）

### 1.2 but — 表示转折

**例句**：
1. He is poor **but** happy. （简单 - 连接两个形容词）
2. I wanted to go to the party, **but** I had too much work to do. （中等 - 连接两个句子）
3. The research findings initially appeared to support the hypothesis, **but** further analysis revealed significant flaws in the methodology that ultimately undermined the validity of the conclusions. （复杂）

### 1.3 or — 表示选择

**例句**：
1. Coffee **or** tea? （简单 - 选择）
2. You can take the bus **or** walk to school; it depends on the weather. （中等）
3. The committee must decide whether to allocate more funding to basic research **or** to redirect resources toward applied projects that are more likely to yield immediate results. （复杂）

### 1.4 so — 表示因果

**例句**：
1. It was raining, **so** I took an umbrella. （简单 - 因果关系）
2. She had been studying for months, **so** she felt confident about the exam. （中等）
3. The experimental conditions were not properly controlled during the initial trials, **so** the researchers had to redesign the entire study, which ultimately delayed the publication of their findings by over a year. （复杂）

### 1.5 yet — 表示转折（但语气比 but 更强）

**例句**：
1. The task is difficult, **yet** achievable. （简单）
2. He has failed many times, **yet** he never gives up. （中等）
3. The technology has been available for decades, **yet** its widespread adoption has been hindered by high costs and a lack of public awareness, **yet** recent government subsidies may finally change this situation. （复杂）

> [!warning] 易错提醒
> - **so** 和 **because** 不能同时使用：
>   - ~~Because it rained, so I stayed home.~~
>   - **Because** it rained, I stayed home. 或 It rained, **so** I stayed home.
> - **but** 和 **although/though** 不能同时使用：
>   - ~~Although he is young, but he is capable.~~
>   - **Although** he is young, he is capable. 或 He is young, **but** he is capable.

## 二、从属连词

### 2.1 引导名词性从句的连词

| 连词 | 引导的从句 | 示例 |
|------|-----------|------|
| **that** | 主语从句/宾语从句/表语从句 | I know **that** he is honest. |
| **whether/if** | 宾语从句（是否） | I wonder **whether** he will come. |
| **what** | 主语从句/宾语从句 | **What** he said surprised me. |
| **how** | 主语从句/宾语从句 | I don't know **how** to do it. |
| **where** | 主语从句/宾语从句 | **Where** he lives is unknown. |
| **when** | 主语从句/宾语从句 | I forgot **when** the meeting starts. |
| **why** | 主语从句/宾语从句 | Tell me **why** you did it. |
| **who/whom** | 主语从句/宾语从句 | I know **who** did it. |

**例句**：
1. I believe **that** he is right. （简单 - that 引导宾语从句）
2. **Whether** we should accept the offer depends on **what** the contract terms actually state. （中等 - whether + what 双从句）
3. **What** the investigation ultimately revealed was **that** the corporation had concealed information about **how** the chemical leak had occurred and **why** no preventive measures had been taken before the incident. （复杂 - 多个从属连词嵌套）

### 2.2 引导状语从句的连词

| 类别 | 连词 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| 时间 | when, while, before, after, since, until, as soon as | **When** I arrived, he had left. |
| 原因 | because, since, as | He stayed home **because** he was sick. |
| 条件 | if, unless, as long as, provided that | **If** it rains, we will stay home. |
| 让步 | although, though, even though, while | **Although** he is young, he is wise. |
| 目的 | so that, in order that | He studies hard **so that** he can pass. |
| 结果 | so...that, such...that | He was **so** tired **that** he fell asleep. |
| 比较 | as...as, than | She is **as** tall **as** her mother. |
| 方式 | as, as if, as though | Do **as** I say. |

**例句**：
1. **If** you study hard, you will succeed. （简单 - 条件从句）
2. **Although** the weather was terrible, we decided to go hiking **because** we had been planning the trip **since** last month. （中等 - 多个状语从句）
3. **While** the government has implemented various policies **so that** small businesses can thrive, **unless** the underlying structural issues are addressed, these measures are unlikely to produce sustainable results, **even though** they may provide temporary relief **as long as** the economic environment remains stable. （复杂 - 大量状语从句嵌套）

> [!warning] 易错提醒
> - **whether** vs. **if** 引导名词性从句时：
>   - 做主语从句：只能用 **whether**（~~If he comes is uncertain.~~）
>   - 做宾语从句：两者都可以
>   - 介词后：只能用 **whether**（It depends on **whether** he agrees.）
>   - 后接 or not：只能用 **whether**（**whether** or not he comes）

> [!tip] 实战技巧
> **阅读中**：
> - 连词是理解句子逻辑关系的关键信号词
> - 看到 although/but → 转折，重点在后面
> - 看到 because/since → 原因，理解因果链
> - 看到 if/unless → 条件，注意假设前提
>
> **写作中**：
> - 用多样化的连词替代反复使用 and/but/so
> - 学术写作中 however, nevertheless, furthermore 等连接副词比并列连词更正式
> - 注意并列结构的对称性：not only A **but also** B（A 和 B 词性一致）
