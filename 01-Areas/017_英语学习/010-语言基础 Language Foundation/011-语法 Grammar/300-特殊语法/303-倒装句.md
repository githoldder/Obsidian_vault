# 倒装句

**核心概括**：倒装句是将正常的"主语 + 谓语"语序变为"谓语（或助动词）+ 主语"的特殊句式，分为全部倒装和部分倒装两种，常因强调、语法要求或修辞需要而使用。

> [!info] 语法规则
> - **全部倒装（Full Inversion）**：整个谓语动词提到主语前面
> - **部分倒装（Partial Inversion）**：只把助动词/情态动词/be动词提到主语前面（半倒装）
> - 部分倒装的结构与一般疑问句语序相同：助动词 + 主语 + 实义动词

---

## 一、部分倒装（半倒装）

### 1.1 否定副词/短语提前

当否定或半否定副词放在句首时，句子要用**部分倒装**。

| 否定副词/短语 | 含义 |
|--------------|------|
| never | 从不 |
| seldom / rarely | 很少 |
| hardly / scarcely | 几乎不 |
| little | 几乎不 |
| not only... but also | 不仅...而且 |
| no sooner... than | 一...就... |
| hardly/scarcely... when | 一...就... |
| not until | 直到...才 |
| nowhere | 没有任何地方 |
| in no way / by no means / under no circumstances | 绝不 |
| at no time | 在任何时候都不 |

**例句**：
1. **Never have** I seen such a beautiful sunset. （简单 - never 提前）
2. **Not only did** she finish the project on time, **but** she **also** received praise from the entire team. （中等 - not only 提前倒装，but also 不倒装）
3. **Hardly had** the chairman announced the new policy **when** protests erupted across the country, and **at no time during** the ensuing debate **did** the government appear willing to reconsider its position, **nor did** it acknowledge the legitimate concerns raised by opposition groups. （复杂 - 多个否定倒装）

> [!warning] 易错提醒
> - **Not only... but also** 结构中，**只有 not only 部分倒装**，but also 部分**不倒装**：
>   - **Not only does** he speak English, **but** he **also** speaks French.（正确）
>   - ~~Not only does he speak English, but also does he speak French.~~（错误）
> - **No sooner... than** 和 **Hardly... when** 的搭配不能混用：
>   - No sooner... **than**（不是 ~~when~~）
>   - Hardly... **when**（不是 ~~than~~）

### 1.2 only + 状语/从句提前

**例句**：
1. **Only then did** I realize my mistake. （简单 - only then 提前）
2. **Only by working** together **can** we solve this complex problem. （中等 - only by doing 提前）
3. **Only when** all the evidence had been thoroughly examined and all the witnesses had been interviewed **did** the detective finally understand the true sequence of events **that** had led to the mysterious disappearance. （复杂 - only when 从句提前）

> [!warning] 易错提醒
> - only 修饰**主语**时**不倒装**：
>   - **Only Tom** can solve this problem.（不倒装 - only 修饰主语）
>   - **Only then** can he solve this problem.（倒装 - only 修饰状语）

### 1.3 so/such 提前

**例句**：
1. **So beautiful was** the scenery **that** we took many photos. （简单 - so + adj. 提前）
2. **So rapidly has** technology advanced in recent years **that** many traditional industries have been completely transformed. （中等 - so + adv. 提前）
3. **So profound was** the impact of the discovery on the field of genetics, and **so far-reaching were** its implications for medical research, **that** the scientific community immediately recognized it as one of the most significant breakthroughs of the century. （复杂 - 两个 so 倒装并列）

### 1.4 as 引导的让步倒装

**结构**：adj./n./v. + **as** + 主语 + 谓语，虽然...

**例句**：
1. **Young as** he is, he is very capable. （简单 - 形容词提前）
2. **Try as** she might, she could not solve the puzzle. （中等 - 动词原形提前）
3. **Impressive as** the preliminary results may appear and **promising as** the theoretical framework seems, the methodology still has **significant** limitations **that** must be addressed before the conclusions can be considered reliable, **much as** the researchers would prefer to present their findings as definitive. （复杂 - 多个 as 让步倒装）

> [!warning] 易错提醒
> - as 让步倒装中，名词提前时要**去掉冠词**：
>   - He is **a** child. → **Child as** he is, he understands a lot.（去掉 a）
>   - ~~A child as he is~~（错误）

### 1.5 虚拟条件句省略 if 的倒装

详见 [[01-Areas/017_英语学习/010-语言基础 Language Foundation/011-语法 Grammar/300-特殊语法/306-虚拟语气]]

**例句**：
1. **Had** I known the truth, I would have told you. （= If I had known...）
2. **Were** she here, she would help us. （= If she were here...）
3. **Should** the project fail to meet its objectives within the specified timeframe, the committee **would** be obligated to reassess the entire strategic framework, and **had** additional funding **been** allocated earlier, such a drastic reassessment **might** not have been necessary. （复杂 - should/had 倒装）

## 二、全部倒装

### 2.1 主系表倒装（表语提前）

当**介词短语、形容词、分词等表语**放在句首时，全部倒装。

**例句**：
1. **On the wall hangs** a beautiful painting. （简单 - 介词短语提前）
2. **Present at** the meeting **were** all the senior managers of the company. （中等 - 形容词短语提前）
3. **Scattered across** the vast desert **were** the ruins of an ancient civilization, and **carved into** the stone walls **were** inscriptions **that** archaeologists believe date back more than three thousand years, **providing** invaluable insights into the daily lives and beliefs of a people long forgotten by history. （复杂 - 多个全部倒装）

### 2.2 there/here/now/then + 动词 + 主语

**例句**：
1. **Here comes** the bus. （简单 - here 提前）
2. **There goes** the last opportunity to resolve this matter peacefully. （中等 - there 提前）
3. **Then came** the moment that everyone had been waiting for — the announcement of the winner. （中等）

> [!warning] 易错提醒
> - 当主语是**代词**时，**不倒装**：
>   - Here comes the bus.（倒装 - 主语是名词）
>   - Here **it** comes.（不倒装 - 主语是代词）
>   - There **he** goes.（不倒装 - 主语是代词）

## 三、倒装句总结表

| 类型 | 条件 | 倒装方式 | 示例 |
|------|------|----------|------|
| 否定词提前 | never/seldom/hardly等 | 部分倒装 | Never **have** I... |
| only + 状语提前 | only then/only when等 | 部分倒装 | Only then **did** I... |
| so/such 提前 | so adj./such n. | 部分倒装 | So tired **was** he... |
| as 让步 | adj./n./v. + as | 部分倒装 | Young **as** he is... |
| 虚拟省略 if | had/were/should | 部分倒装 | **Had** I known... |
| 表语提前 | 介词短语/adj. 提前 | 全部倒装 | On the wall **hangs**... |
| there/here 句首 | there/here/then + 动词 | 全部倒装 | Here **comes**... |

> [!tip] 实战技巧
> **阅读中**：
> - 遇到倒装句，先还原为正常语序理解句意
> - 关键信号词：never, seldom, hardly, not only, only, so, such, as → 可能有倒装
>
> **写作中**：
> - 用 Not only...but also 倒装开头增加句式多样性
> - 用 Only by doing...can we 结构强调方法
> - 学术写作中用 So significant were the findings that... 增强表达力
> - 适度使用，过多倒装会使文章显得刻意
