# Day05 昂克英文君：吞音 Glottal Stop（喉音停顿）

## 视频
[【最全英语连读规则】(05 吞音 Glottal stop 喉音停顿)](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1jE41157bX?p=5)

## 核心规则
/t/ 或 /d/ 变成喉音停顿 /ʔ/

**written → wri-ʔn**
**certain → cer-ʔn**
**mountain → moun-ʔn**
**couldn't → cou-ʔn't**
