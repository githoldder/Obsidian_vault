# 非谓语从句

**核心概括**：非谓语结构（doing/done/to do）是从句的简化形式，用来替代定语从句或状语从句，使句子更简洁；无逗号时做后置定语，有逗号时做状语。

> [!info] 语法规则
> - **无逗号** → 非谓语做**后置定语**，修饰前面的名词（相当于定语从句的简化）
> - **有逗号** → 非谓语做**状语**，修饰整个句子（相当于状语从句的简化）
> - **doing** = 主动/正在进行
> - **done** = 被动/已完成
> - **to do** = 目的/将来/主动

---

## 一、无逗号 — 做后置定语

非谓语结构紧跟名词后面，修饰该名词，相当于定语从句的简化。

### 1.1 doing 做后置定语（主动/正在进行）

**从句 → 非谓语**：
- The man **who is standing** at the door → The man **standing** at the door

**例句**：
1. The girl **sitting** next to me is my classmate. （简单 - = who is sitting）
2. The company **providing** cloud computing services has experienced rapid growth this year. （中等 - = which provides）
3. The data **suggesting** a strong correlation between sleep deprivation and cognitive decline have prompted researchers **working** in the field of neuroscience to conduct more extensive studies **examining** the long-term effects on brain function. （复杂 - 多个 doing 后置定语）

### 1.2 done 做后置定语（被动/已完成）

**从句 → 非谓语**：
- The book **which was written** by him → The book **written** by him

**例句**：
1. The letter **written** in English is from my pen pal. （简单 - = which was written）
2. The policies **implemented** by the government last year have significantly **reduced** pollution levels. （中等 - = which were implemented）
3. The evidence **collected** during the investigation, combined with the testimony **provided** by multiple witnesses and the documents **recovered** from the suspect's computer, ultimately led to a conviction on all charges **filed** against the defendant. （复杂 - 多个 done 后置定语）

### 1.3 to do 做后置定语（将来/目的）

**从句 → 非谓语**：
- I have a lot of work **that I need to do** → I have a lot of work **to do**

**例句**：
1. She is the best person **to handle** this job. （简单 - = who can handle）
2. The government announced a new plan **to reduce** carbon emissions by 50% over the next decade. （中等 - = which aims to reduce）
3. The committee has developed a comprehensive strategy **to address** the root causes of inequality, incorporating specific measures **to improve** access to education and **to create** more employment opportunities in underserved communities. （复杂 - 多个 to do 后置定语）

## 二、有逗号 — 做状语

非谓语结构用逗号与主句隔开，做状语修饰整个句子。

### 2.1 doing 做状语（主动）

表示**时间、原因、伴随、结果**。

> [!info] 语法规则
> doing 的逻辑主语必须是**句子的主语**，两者之间是主动关系。

**例句**：
1. **Hearing** the news, she burst into tears. （简单 - 时间状语：当她听到消息时）
2. **Not knowing** what to do, he sat there in silence, **waiting** for someone to help. （中等 - 原因 + 伴随状语）
3. **Having analyzed** the data from multiple perspectives and **having consulted** with experts in related fields, the research team published their findings in a leading journal, **attracting** widespread attention from both the academic community and the general public, **ultimately leading** to a fundamental revision of the existing theoretical framework. （复杂 - 完成式做时间状语 + doing 做结果状语）

### 2.2 done 做状语（被动）

表示**时间、原因、条件**，逻辑主语与句子主语之间是被动关系。

**例句**：
1. **Seen** from the hill, the city looks beautiful. （简单 - 条件状语：从山上看）
2. **Encouraged** by the positive feedback, she decided to continue her research. （中等 - 原因状语）
3. **Overwhelmed** by the complexity of the situation and **frustrated** by the lack of support from senior management, the project leader submitted her resignation, which, **viewed** in retrospect, proved to be a decision that **benefited** both her career and the organization in the long run. （复杂 - 多个 done 做状语）

### 2.3 to do 做状语（目的）

**例句**：
1. He went to the library **to borrow** some books. （简单 - 目的状语）
2. **To improve** air quality, the government has banned the use of coal in residential areas. （中等 - 目的状语前置）
3. **To ensure** that the transition to renewable energy sources proceeds smoothly and **to minimize** the economic disruption that inevitably accompanies such fundamental changes, the government has allocated substantial funding for retraining programs designed to help workers in traditional industries acquire new skills. （复杂 - 多个 to do 目的状语）

## 三、三种非谓语形式的对比

| | doing | done | to do |
|---|-------|------|-------|
| 语态 | 主动 | 被动 | 主动 |
| 时间 | 正在/同时 | 已完成 | 将来/目的 |
| 做定语 | 正在做的（主动） | 被做的（被动） | 将要做的 |
| 做状语 | 主动动作 | 被动动作 | 目的 |

**对比例句**：
- the man **calling** you（正在打电话给你的人 - 主动进行）
- the man **called** Tom（被叫做 Tom 的人 - 被动）
- the man **to call** you（将要打电话给你的人 - 将来）

## 四、非谓语结构 vs. 完整从句

| 从句形式 | 非谓语简化 |
|----------|-----------|
| The man **who is reading** a book... | The man **reading** a book... |
| The bridge **which was built** last year... | The bridge **built** last year... |
| **Because she didn't know** the answer... | **Not knowing** the answer... |
| **After he had finished** his work... | **Having finished** his work... |
| **Because it was seen** from above... | **Seen** from above... |

> [!warning] 易错提醒
> - 非谓语做状语时，其逻辑主语**必须与句子主语一致**（悬垂分词错误）：
>   - ~~**Walking** down the street, **the building** caught my eye.~~（建筑不能走路）
>   - **Walking** down the street, **I** noticed a beautiful building.（正确）
> - 如果逻辑主语不同，必须保留从句形式或使用**独立主格结构**：
>   - **The weather being** fine, we went for a walk.（独立主格）

> [!tip] 实战技巧
> **阅读中**：
> - 非谓语结构是长难句的重要组成部分，关键在于判断其修饰对象
> - 无逗号 → 找前面的名词（做定语）
> - 有逗号 → 修饰整个主句（做状语），找句子主语判断主被动关系
>
> **写作中**：
> - 用非谓语替代从句使句子更简洁精炼
> - 学术写作中，done 做后置定语非常常见：the results **obtained** from...
> - 注意主被动关系的选择：主语发出动作用 doing，承受动作用 done
