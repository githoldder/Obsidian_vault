# there be 句型

**核心概括**：there be 句型表示"某处存在某物/某人"，是英语中表达存在关系的核心结构；be 动词的单复数取决于后面紧跟的名词（就近原则），后面可接多种定语形式。

> [!info] 语法规则
> - 基本结构：**There + be + 主语 + 地点/时间状语**
> - be 动词的形式取决于紧跟其后的名词（**就近原则**）
> - there be 不能与 have 混用
> - there be 后可接形容词、介词短语、定语从句、非谓语等做后置定语

---

## 一、基本结构与时态变化

| 时态 | 结构 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| 一般现在时 | there is/are | There **is** a book on the desk. |
| 一般过去时 | there was/were | There **were** many people at the party. |
| 一般将来时 | there will be | There **will be** a meeting tomorrow. |
| 现在完成时 | there has/have been | There **have been** many changes. |
| 现在进行时 | there is/are + being | — （较少用） |
| 情态动词 | there may/must/can be | There **must be** a mistake. |

**例句**：
1. There **is** a cat under the table. （简单 - 一般现在时）
2. There **have been** significant improvements in air quality since the new regulations **were** introduced. （中等 - 现在完成时）
3. There **will inevitably be** challenges and setbacks along the way, but as long as there **is** determination and there **are** resources available, there **should be** no reason why the project cannot succeed within the anticipated timeframe. （复杂 - 多种时态和情态动词）

---

## 二、就近原则

> [!info] 语法规则
> 当 there be 后面有多个并列主语时，be 动词的形式取决于**最近的那个名词**。

**例句**：
1. There **is** a pen and two books on the desk. （简单 - 就近 a pen → is）
2. There **are** three students and a teacher in the classroom. （简单 - 就近 three students → are）
3. There **was** a significant decrease in revenue and multiple complaints from customers during the period when the new system **was** being tested, which raised concerns about **whether** there **were** fundamental design flaws that had been overlooked. （复杂）

---

## 三、there be 后接定语的形式

这是 there be 句型的核心难点。主语名词后面可以接多种形式的定语。

### 3.1 形容词做后置定语

**例句**：
1. There is nothing **wrong**. （简单 - 不定代词后接形容词）
2. There is something **important** that I need to tell you. （中等）
3. Is there anything **fundamentally different** about the approach used in this study compared to anything **similar** that has been attempted before? （复杂）

### 3.2 介词短语做后置定语

**例句**：
1. There is a book **on the table**. （简单）
2. There are several factories **along the river** that have been operating **without proper** permits. （中等）
3. There are numerous communities **in developing countries** **without access to** clean water, and there remain significant gaps **in the infrastructure** necessary **for the delivery of** basic healthcare services **to remote areas**. （复杂 - 多个介词短语层叠）

### 3.3 定语从句做后置定语

**例句**：
1. There is a man **who wants to see you**. （简单）
2. There are many factors **that** contribute to the success of a business. （中等）
3. There is growing evidence **that** suggests a strong correlation between physical activity and mental health, and there are also studies **which** indicate **that** the benefits extend to cognitive function, **which** is particularly relevant for populations **in which** age-related decline is a concern. （复杂 - 多个定语从句）

### 3.4 非谓语做后置定语

| 形式 | 含义 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| doing | 主动/正在进行 | There is a girl **singing** in the room. |
| done | 被动/已完成 | There are many problems **left** to solve. |
| to do | 将要/目的 | There is a lot of work **to do**. |

**例句**：
1. There is a boy **standing** at the door. （简单 - doing 主动）
2. There are several issues **remaining** to be resolved before the deadline. （中等 - doing 主动）
3. There are numerous abandoned buildings **scattered** across the old industrial district, with environmental hazards **waiting** to be addressed and regulatory requirements **designed** to protect public safety still **needing** to be enforced by local authorities **overwhelmed** by limited budgets and staff. （复杂 - 多种非谓语形式混合）

## 四、there be 与 have 的区别

| | there be | have/has |
|---|---------|---------|
| 含义 | 某处**存在**某物 | 某人**拥有**某物 |
| 主语 | 后置主语（事物） | 前置主语（人） |
| 强调 | 存在关系 | 所属关系 |

> [!warning] 易错提醒
> - there be 和 have **不能混用**：
>   - ~~There has a book on the desk.~~（错误）
>   - There **is** a book on the desk.（正确 - 存在）
>   - I **have** a book.（正确 - 拥有）
> - there be 句型中的 there 是**引导词**，不是"那里"的意思
> - 否定形式：There **is no** / There **isn't any**
>   - There is **no** water in the bottle. = There **isn't any** water in the bottle.

## 五、there be 的扩展结构

除了 be 动词，there 后面还可以接其他表示"存在/出现/发生"的动词：

| 结构 | 含义 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| there exist(s) | 存在 | There **exists** a solution to this problem. |
| there remain(s) | 仍然存在 | There **remain** many challenges. |
| there seem(s) to be | 似乎有 | There **seems to be** a problem. |
| there happen(s) to be | 碰巧有 | There **happened to be** a doctor nearby. |
| there stand(s) | 矗立着 | There **stands** an ancient temple on the hill. |
| there lie(s) | 躺着/位于 | There **lies** a beautiful lake behind the mountain. |

**例句**：
1. There **seems** to be a misunderstanding. （简单）
2. There **remain** several unresolved issues that must be addressed before the project can proceed. （中等）
3. On the outskirts of the ancient city, there **stands** a monument dedicated to those who sacrificed their lives during the war, and nearby there **lies** a garden where there still **exist** species of plants that **were** believed to have been extinct for centuries. （复杂 - 多种存在动词）

> [!tip] 实战技巧
> **阅读中**：
> - there be 句型重点看 be 后面的名词（真正的主语）以及修饰它的定语
> - 在学术文章中，there be 后常接长定语从句或非谓语，要耐心找到主语
>
> **写作中**：
> - 用 there be 引入新信息，是段落开头的好方式
> - 学术写作中善用 there exist(s)/there remain(s) 替代 there is/are，更正式
> - 避免过度使用 there be，可以换用主动句式使文章更有力
