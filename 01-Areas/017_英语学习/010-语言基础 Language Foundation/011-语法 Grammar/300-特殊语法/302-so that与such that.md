# so that 与 such that

**核心概括**：so...that 和 such...that 都表示"如此...以至于..."，区别在于 so 后接形容词/副词，such 后接名词；而 so that 作为连词则表示"以便/为了"，引导目的状语从句。

> [!info] 语法规则
> - **so + adj./adv. + that** → 如此（形容词/副词）以至于...（结果）
> - **such + (a/an) + adj. + n. + that** → 如此（名词短语）以至于...（结果）
> - **so that** → 以便/为了（目的），从句常含 can/could/may/might

---

## 一、so...that 结构（so + adj./adv. + that）

### 1.1 so + 形容词 + that

**例句**：
1. He was **so** tired **that** he fell asleep immediately. （简单）
2. The exam was **so** difficult **that** more than half of the students failed to pass it. （中等）
3. The pollution in the river had become **so** severe over the past two decades **that** not only had all aquatic life disappeared, but the contaminated water had also begun to affect the health of residents in surrounding communities, **so** much **so that** the government was forced to declare the entire area a public health emergency zone. （复杂 - so...that + so much so that）

### 1.2 so + 副词 + that

**例句**：
1. She spoke **so** quickly **that** I couldn't understand her. （简单）
2. The technology has advanced **so** rapidly **that** many workers have been unable to keep up with the changes. （中等）
3. The population of the city had grown **so** dramatically and the infrastructure had deteriorated **so** significantly **that** the municipal government found itself completely overwhelmed by the simultaneous demands for housing, transportation, healthcare, and educational services. （复杂 - 两个 so...that 并列）

### 1.3 so + many/much/few/little + n. + that

> [!info] 语法规则
> 当 so 修饰的是 many/much/few/little + 名词时，用 so 而非 such：

| 搭配 | 示例 |
|------|------|
| so **many** + 可数名词复数 | There were **so many** people **that** we couldn't find a seat. |
| so **much** + 不可数名词 | He has **so much** work **that** he can't sleep. |
| so **few** + 可数名词复数 | There are **so few** options **that** we have no choice. |
| so **little** + 不可数名词 | We had **so little** time **that** we couldn't finish. |

**例句**：
1. There were **so many** cars **that** the traffic was terrible. （简单）
2. She had **so much** experience **that** she was immediately offered the position. （中等）
3. The investigation uncovered **so many** irregularities and revealed **so much** evidence of systematic corruption **that** the entire board of directors was replaced, leaving **so few** senior executives in place **that** the company struggled to maintain normal operations for months. （复杂 - so many/much/few 并用）

## 二、such...that 结构（such + n. + that）

### 2.1 such + a/an + adj. + 可数名词单数 + that

**例句**：
1. It was **such** a hot day **that** we decided to go swimming. （简单）
2. She is **such** an excellent teacher **that** all her students love and respect her. （中等）
3. The discovery represented **such** a fundamental breakthrough in our understanding of cellular biology **that** it not only earned the lead researcher a Nobel Prize but also opened up entirely new avenues of investigation **that** had previously been considered impossible. （复杂）

### 2.2 such + adj. + 不可数名词/可数名词复数 + that

**例句**：
1. They are **such** lovely children **that** everyone likes them. （简单 - 可数复数）
2. He showed **such** great courage **that** he was praised by everyone. （中等 - 不可数名词）
3. The experiment produced **such** unexpected results and generated **such** intense debate within the scientific community **that** several leading journals published special issues dedicated to examining the methodological implications and the broader theoretical questions **that** the findings raised. （复杂）

## 三、so...that vs. such...that 对比

| | so...that | such...that |
|---|-----------|-------------|
| 后接 | adj./adv. | (a/an) + adj. + n. |
| 核心词 | 形容词/副词 | 名词（短语） |
| 示例 | **so** beautiful **that** | **such** a beautiful girl **that** |
| many/much/few/little | 用 **so** | 不用 such |

### 互换规则

- **such** a beautiful girl **that**... = **so** beautiful a girl **that**...
- 注意：so + adj. + **a/an** + n.（冠词位置不同）

**例句对比**：
1. She is **such** a kind person **that** everyone likes her. = She is **so** kind a person **that** everyone likes her. （简单）
2. It was **such** terrible weather **that** we stayed indoors. = The weather was **so** terrible **that** we stayed indoors. （中等 - such + 不可数名词 对比 so + 形容词）

> [!warning] 易错提醒
> - **so** 后面不能直接接名词：~~so a beautiful girl~~ → **such** a beautiful girl 或 **so** beautiful a girl
> - **such** 后面不能直接接形容词（没有名词）：~~such beautiful~~ → **so** beautiful
> - 当名词前有 many/much/few/little 时，用 **so** 不用 such：
>   - **so** many people（不是 ~~such many people~~）
>   - **so** much money（不是 ~~such much money~~）

## 四、so that 作为目的连词

> [!info] 语法规则
> **so that** 做连词时表示"以便/为了"，引导**目的状语从句**，从句中常含 can/could/may/might/will/would。

**例句**：
1. He studied hard **so that** he could pass the exam. （简单 - 目的）
2. Please speak louder **so that** everyone in the room can hear you clearly. （中等）
3. The government established a comprehensive scholarship program **so that** students from underprivileged backgrounds **could** gain access to higher education, and it simultaneously invested in digital infrastructure **so that** remote communities **might** benefit from online learning platforms that were previously unavailable to them. （复杂 - 两个 so that 目的从句）

### so that 目的 vs. so...that 结果 的区分

| | so that（目的） | so...that（结果） |
|---|----------------|-----------------|
| 含义 | 为了/以便 | 如此...以至于 |
| 从句特征 | 常含 can/could/may/might | 描述实际结果 |
| 时态 | 从句动作未发生 | 从句动作已发生 |
| so 与 that 之间 | 无其他成分 | 有 adj./adv. |

**对比例句**：
- He spoke loudly **so that** everyone **could** hear him.（目的 - 为了大家能听到）
- He spoke **so** loudly **that** everyone heard him.（结果 - 说得太大声以至于大家都听到了）

> [!tip] 实战技巧
> **阅读中**：
> - 看到 so/such...that，快速判断是结果关系，重点理解 that 后的结果
> - 看到 so that（中间无adj.），判断是目的关系，看从句中有无情态动词
>
> **写作中**：
> - 用 so...that/such...that 表达因果关系，比单纯用 because/so 更具表现力
> - 学术写作中用 so that 表达研究目的：The experiment was designed so that...
> - 注意 so...that 可以引起倒装（详见 [[01-Areas/017_英语学习/010-语言基础 Language Foundation/011-语法 Grammar/300-特殊语法/303-倒装句]]）
