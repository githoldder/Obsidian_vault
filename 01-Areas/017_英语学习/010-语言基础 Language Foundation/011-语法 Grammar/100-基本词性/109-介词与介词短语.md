# 介词与介词短语

**核心概括**：介词是连接名词（或代词、动名词）与句子其他部分的桥梁，表示时间、地点、方式、原因等关系；介词 + 名词/代词/doing 构成介词短语，可在句中做定语、状语或表语。

> [!info] 语法规则
> - 介词后面必须接**名词、代词或动名词（doing）**，不能接动词原形
> - 介词 + 宾语 = 介词短语
> - 介词短语可做**定语**（修饰名词）、**状语**（修饰动词/句子）、**表语**（跟在系动词后）
> - 介词后的代词必须用**宾格**（to **him**, for **me**）

---

## 一、常用介词及核心含义

### 1.1 时间介词 in / on / at

| 介词 | 用于 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| **in** | 年/月/季节/世纪/较长时间段 | in 2026, in January, in summer, in the morning |
| **on** | 具体某天/日期/星期 | on Monday, on July 4th, on Christmas Day |
| **at** | 具体时刻/特定时间点 | at 8 o'clock, at noon, at night, at the weekend |

**例句**：
1. I was born **in** 1998. / The meeting is **on** Monday. / Class starts **at** 9 a.m. （简单）
2. She always goes jogging **in** the morning **on** weekdays, but **at** the weekend she prefers to sleep **in**. （中等 - 三个时间介词对比）
3. **In** the early decades of the 21st century, particularly **on** the days following major technological breakthroughs, investors would gather **at** specific times to analyze market trends that had emerged **in** the previous quarter. （复杂）

### 1.2 地点介词 in / on / at

| 介词 | 用于 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| **in** | 在...里面（三维空间） | in the box, in China, in the room |
| **on** | 在...上面（表面接触） | on the table, on the wall, on the bus |
| **at** | 在...处（具体地点/位置） | at the door, at school, at the airport |

**例句**：
1. The book is **on** the table. （简单）
2. She is studying **at** the library, which is located **in** the center of the city **on** Main Street. （中等）
3. The conference was held **at** a prestigious hotel **in** downtown Tokyo, where delegates from organizations **in** over 50 countries gathered **on** the third floor to discuss policies that would be implemented **in** their respective regions. （复杂）

### 1.3 其他高频介词

| 介词 | 核心含义 | 常见搭配 |
|------|----------|----------|
| **by** | 通过/被/截止/在旁边 | by bus, by hand, written by, by the window, by Friday |
| **with** | 和/用/具有 | with friends, with a knife, a girl with blue eyes |
| **to** | 到/向/给 | go to school, listen to, give it to me |
| **for** | 为了/持续/因为 | for you, for three days, famous for |
| **against** | 反对/靠着 | against the wall, fight against |
| **up** | 向上/沿着 | go up, walk up the hill |
| **from** | 从/来自 | from China, from 9 to 5 |
| **about** | 关于/大约 | talk about, about 100 people |
| **between** | 在两者之间 | between A and B |
| **among** | 在三者或以上之间 | among the students |

**例句**：
1. She went to school **by** bus. （简单）
2. The conflict **between** the two countries lasted **for** decades and was fought **against** a backdrop of ideological differences. （中等）
3. **Among** all the proposals submitted **to** the committee **by** the deadline, the one **from** the environmental group was selected **for** its innovative approach **to** addressing the challenges faced **by** coastal communities **against** rising sea levels. （复杂 - 多介词串联）

## 二、介词 + 名词/doing 的固定搭配

### 2.1 介词 + doing（动名词）

> [!info] 语法规则
> 介词后面的动词必须用 **-ing 形式**（动名词），不能用动词原形或不定式。

| 搭配 | 示例 |
|------|------|
| be good at **doing** | She is good at **singing**. |
| be interested in **doing** | I am interested in **learning** Chinese. |
| look forward to **doing** | I look forward to **hearing** from you. |
| be used to **doing** | He is used to **getting** up early. |
| instead of **doing** | Instead of **complaining**, take action. |
| without **doing** | He left without **saying** goodbye. |
| by **doing** | You can improve by **practicing** every day. |

**例句**：
1. She is good at **playing** the piano. （简单）
2. Instead of **wasting** time on social media, you should focus on **improving** your skills. （中等）
3. By **conducting** extensive field research and **analyzing** data from multiple sources without **relying** solely on theoretical models, the scientists succeeded in **identifying** previously unknown species in the region. （复杂）

> [!warning] 易错提醒
> - **look forward to** 中的 to 是**介词**，后接 doing：
>   - I look forward to **meeting** you.（不是 ~~to meet~~）
> - **be used to** 中的 to 是**介词**，后接 doing：
>   - I am used to **living** alone.（习惯于独居）
>   - 区别：I **used to** live alone.（过去常常独居 - used to 是固定结构，接原形）
> - **pay attention to** 中的 to 是**介词**：
>   - Pay attention to **listening**.（不是 ~~to listen~~）

## 三、介词短语的句法功能

### 3.1 做定语（修饰名词，后置）

**例句**：
1. The girl **in red** is my sister. （简单 - in red 修饰 girl）
2. The book **on the top shelf** is the one I have been looking for. （中等）
3. The challenges **facing** communities **in developing countries** **without access to** clean water remain one of the most pressing issues **of** our time. （复杂 - 多个介词短语做定语）

### 3.2 做状语（修饰动词/整个句子）

**例句**：
1. She arrived **at 8 o'clock**. （简单 - 时间状语）
2. He solved the problem **with great patience** and **in a creative way**. （中等 - 方式状语）
3. **In spite of** the numerous obstacles encountered **during** the research process, the team managed to publish their findings **in** a leading journal **within** the original timeframe **by working** collaboratively **across** multiple departments. （复杂 - 多个介词短语做不同类型状语）

### 3.3 做表语（跟在系动词后）

**例句**：
1. The meeting is **at 3 p.m.** （简单）
2. The problem is **beyond** our control. （中等）
3. The root cause of the failure was **in** the fundamental assumptions **of** the model rather than **in** the quality **of** the data used **for** calibration. （复杂）

> [!tip] 实战技巧
> **阅读中**：
> - 介词短语做后置定语时，快速判断它修饰前面的哪个名词
> - 长难句中大量介词短语层层嵌套是难点，从最内层开始理解
>
> **写作中**：
> - 善用介词短语做后置定语，使表达更精确
> - 避免介词堆砌导致句子晦涩，一个句子中不宜超过4个介词短语
> - 注意固定搭配中的介词选择：depend **on**, consist **of**, result **in/from**
