# with 复合结构

**核心概括**：with 复合结构由 "with + 名词/代词 + 补语" 构成，在句中做状语或定语，表示伴随、原因、条件等；独立主格结构与其功能类似，但不使用 with。

> [!info] 语法规则
> - **with + 名词/代词 + doing**（主动/正在进行）
> - **with + 名词/代词 + done**（被动/已完成）
> - **with + 名词/代词 + to do**（将来/目的）
> - **with + 名词/代词 + adj./adv./介词短语**（状态描述）
> - with 结构通常做**伴随状语、原因状语或后置定语**

---

## 一、with + n. + doing（主动/正在进行）

名词与 doing 之间是**主动关系**，表示名词正在做某事。

**例句**：
1. She sat there, **with** tears **running** down her face. （简单 - 伴随状语：眼泪正在流下）
2. He walked into the room **with** his dog **following** closely behind. （中等 - 伴随状语）
3. **With** the global economy **undergoing** a fundamental transformation and technological innovation **disrupting** traditional industries at an unprecedented pace, governments around the world face the challenge of adapting their educational systems to prepare the workforce for a future that remains largely unpredictable. （复杂 - 原因/背景状语）

---

## 二、with + n. + done（被动/已完成）

名词与 done 之间是**被动关系**，表示名词被做了某事或某事已完成。

**例句**：
1. He sat in the chair **with** his eyes **closed**. （简单 - 伴随状语：眼睛被闭着）
2. **With** the work **finished**, they went home happily. （中等 - 原因/时间状语：工作完成了）
3. **With** all the preliminary data **collected**, the statistical models **calibrated**, and the research protocols **approved** by the ethics committee, the team was finally ready to begin the most critical phase of the experiment that they had spent over two years preparing for. （复杂 - 多个 done 并列）

---

## 三、with + n. + to do（将来/尚未完成）

名词与 to do 之间表示**将要做的事**或**目的**。

**例句**：
1. **With** so much work **to do**, I can't go out tonight. （简单 - 原因：有很多工作要做）
2. **With** the deadline **to meet** and several reports **to write**, she had no choice but to work overtime. （中等 - 多个 to do 并列）
3. **With** a comprehensive reform agenda **to implement**, limited resources **to allocate** among competing priorities, and growing public expectations **to manage**, the newly elected government faced a daunting set of challenges during its first year in office. （复杂 - 多个 to do 并列描述挑战）

---

## 四、with + n. + adj./adv./介词短语

名词后接形容词、副词或介词短语，描述名词的状态。

**例句**：
1. Don't speak **with** your mouth **full**. （简单 - adj. 描述状态：嘴里满的）
2. He stood at the door **with** his hands **in his pockets**. （中等 - 介词短语描述状态）
3. The old man sat quietly on the bench **with** his back **straight**, his eyes **fixed on** the distant horizon, and a faint smile **on** his weathered face, seemingly lost in memories of a time long past. （复杂 - 多种补语形式混合）

---

## 五、with 结构的句法功能

| 功能 | 示例 |
|------|------|
| 伴随状语 | He spoke **with** confidence **shining** in his eyes. |
| 原因状语 | **With** the exam **approaching**, students became nervous. |
| 条件状语 | **With** your help, I can finish it on time. |
| 后置定语 | The girl **with** blue eyes is my sister. |
| 方式状语 | She left the room **with** the door **open**. |

---

## 六、独立主格结构

独立主格结构与 with 复合结构功能相同，但**不使用 with**，直接用"名词/代词 + 分词/形容词/介词短语"。

> [!info] 语法规则
> - 独立主格的逻辑主语与句子主语**不同**
> - 结构：**名词/代词 + doing/done/adj./介词短语/to do**
> - 功能：做状语（伴随/原因/条件/时间）

### 6.1 名词 + doing（主动）

**例句**：
1. **Weather permitting**, we will go on a picnic. （简单 - 条件：如果天气允许）
2. **Time permitting**, I would like to visit a few more museums during this trip. （中等）
3. **The sun having set** behind the mountains and **darkness rapidly descending** upon the valley, the exhausted hikers decided to set up camp by the river rather than risk navigating the treacherous mountain path in the fading light. （复杂 - 完成式独立主格 + 普通独立主格）

### 6.2 名词 + done（被动）

**例句**：
1. **The meeting over**, everyone left the room. （简单 - 时间：会议结束后）
2. **All things considered**, this is the best option we have. （中等 - 条件/背景）
3. **The negotiations having been concluded** and **a formal agreement signed** by representatives from both parties, the two companies announced a strategic partnership that would combine their respective strengths in technology and market presence. （复杂 - 完成被动式独立主格）

### 独立主格 vs. with 结构对比

| | 独立主格 | with 结构 |
|---|---------|-----------|
| 引导词 | 无 | 有 with |
| 正式程度 | 更正式/书面 | 较口语化 |
| 示例 | **Weather permitting**, we... | **With** the weather **permitting**, we... |
| 本质 | 相同 | 相同 |

> [!warning] 易错提醒
> - with 结构和独立主格结构的逻辑主语与句子主语**不同**（这是它们存在的原因）
> - 如果逻辑主语与句子主语**相同**，直接用分词做状语即可：
>   - **Walking** down the street, I met Tom.（主语都是 I，直接用分词）
>   - ~~**With** me **walking** down the street, I met Tom.~~（不需要用 with）
> - **doing** vs. **done** 的选择取决于名词与动作的主被动关系：
>   - **With** the baby **sleeping**...（婴儿主动在睡 → doing）
>   - **With** the door **locked**...（门被锁了 → done）

> [!tip] 实战技巧
> **阅读中**：
> - with 结构常出现在长难句中，快速判断 with 后名词与补语的关系（主动/被动/状态）
> - 独立主格结构在学术论文中常见，识别其边界（到逗号处结束）
>
> **写作中**：
> - 用 with 结构添加细节描写：She stood there, with a book in her hand.
> - 用独立主格表达条件/原因更正式：All things considered, Weather permitting
> - 用 with + done 表达完成后的状态：With the work done, we relaxed.
> - 替代状语从句使句子更简洁：After the meeting ended → The meeting over / With the meeting over
