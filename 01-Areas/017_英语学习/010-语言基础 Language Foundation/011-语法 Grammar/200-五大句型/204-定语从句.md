# 定语从句

**核心概括**：定语从句（也称关系从句）是修饰名词或代词的从句，由关系代词（who/whom/which/that/whose）或关系副词（where/when/why）引导，紧跟在被修饰的名词（先行词）之后。

> [!info] 语法规则
> - 定语从句的位置：紧跟在**先行词**（被修饰的名词）之后
> - 关系代词在从句中做**主语、宾语或定语**
> - 关系副词在从句中做**状语**
> - 分为**限制性定语从句**（不加逗号，不可省略）和**非限制性定语从句**（加逗号，可省略）

---

## 一、限制性定语从句

限制性定语从句对先行词起限定作用，去掉后句意不完整或改变。

### 1.1 关系代词的选择

| 先行词 | 做主语        | 做宾语                | 做定语            |
| --- | ---------- | ------------------ | -------------- |
| 人   | who/that   | who/whom/that（可省略） | whose          |
| 物   | which/that | which/that（可省略）    | whose/of which |
| 人+物 | that       | that（可省略）          | whose          |

**例句**：
1. The man **who/that** is standing there is my father. （简单 - who 做主语，指人）
2. The book **(which/that)** I bought yesterday is very interesting. （中等 - which 做宾语，可省略）
3. The scientist **whose** groundbreaking research **that** combined artificial intelligence with traditional biological methods has attracted significant attention from both the academic community and the private sector recently received a prestigious international award. （复杂 - whose 做定语）

### 1.2 只能用 that 的情况

| 情况 | 示例 |
|------|------|
| 先行词是 all, everything, anything, nothing 等不定代词 | **Everything that** he said is true. |
| 先行词被最高级修饰 | This is the best movie **that** I have ever seen. |
| 先行词被序数词修饰 | He is the first person **that** arrived. |
| 先行词被 the only, the very, the same 修饰 | This is the only book **that** I need. |
| 先行词既有人又有物 | The man and his dog **that** passed by... |

**例句**：
1. Is there anything **that** I can do for you? （简单）
2. This is the most beautiful painting **that** I have ever seen in my life. （中等）
3. The only strategy **that** proved effective during the crisis was the one **that** combined immediate financial relief with long-term structural reforms, and it was the first approach **that** received unanimous support from all committee members. （复杂 - 多个 that 从句）

> [!warning] 易错提醒
> - 以上情况下**不能用 which**，只能用 **that**
> - 反过来，**非限制性定语从句中不能用 that**，只能用 which/who

---

## 二、非限制性定语从句

非限制性定语从句用**逗号**与主句隔开，对先行词起补充说明作用，去掉不影响句意。

> [!info] 语法规则
> - 非限制性定语从句只能用 **which**（指物）、**who/whom**（指人），**不能用 that**
> - **which** 可以指代前面整个句子
> - 翻译时通常另起一句

**例句**：
1. Beijing, **which** is the capital of China, has a long history. （简单 - which 指代 Beijing）
2. She passed the exam, **which** made her parents very happy. （中等 - which 指代前面整件事）
3. Professor Li, **who** has devoted over three decades to the study of quantum computing, **which** is widely regarded as one of the most promising fields in modern physics, recently published a paper in Nature, **which** immediately attracted worldwide attention and sparked intense debate **among** researchers. （复杂 - 多个非限制性从句嵌套）

### 限制性 vs. 非限制性对比

| | 限制性 | 非限制性 |
|---|--------|---------|
| 逗号 | 无 | 有 |
| 功能 | 限定/识别先行词 | 补充说明 |
| 省略后 | 句意不完整 | 句意仍完整 |
| that | 可用 | 不可用 |
| 指代整句 | 不可 | which 可以 |

**对比例句**：
- I have a brother **who** lives in Beijing.（限制性 - 我有不止一个兄弟，住在北京的那个）
- I have a brother, **who** lives in Beijing.（非限制性 - 我有一个兄弟，他住在北京）

## 三、带介词的定语从句

关系代词前面可以加介词，形成 "介词 + which/whom" 结构。

> [!info] 语法规则
> - 介词 + **which**（指物）
> - 介词 + **whom**（指人）
> - 介词提前后，**不能用 that** 替代 which/whom
> - 介词的选择取决于从句中动词/形容词的搭配

**例句**：
1. This is the house **in which** he lives. = This is the house **which** he lives **in**. （简单）
2. The person **to whom** I spoke yesterday is the manager of this department. （中等）
3. The theoretical framework **on which** the entire research methodology was based, and the statistical tools **with which** the data were analyzed, have both been called into question by subsequent studies, the findings **of which** suggest that the original conclusions may have been fundamentally flawed. （复杂 - 多个 "介词+which" 结构）

常见搭配：

| 动词/形容词搭配         | 定语从句形式                                    |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| live in          | the house **in which** he lives           |
| depend on        | the factor **on which** it depends        |
| be proud of      | the achievement **of which** she is proud |
| look after       | the child **after whom** she looks        |
| be familiar with | the topic **with which** he is familiar   |

## 四、关系副词引导的定语从句

| 关系副词      | 先行词    | 相当于            | 示例                            |
| --------- | ------ | -------------- | ----------------------------- |
| **where** | 地点名词   | in/at/on which | the city **where** I was born |
| **when**  | 时间名词   | in/on/at which | the day **when** we met       |
| **why**   | reason | for which      | the reason **why** he left    |

**例句**：
1. This is the city **where** I was born. （简单）
2. I still remember the day **when** we first met at the school library. （中等）
3. The researchers investigated the reasons **why** certain communities adopted new technologies more rapidly than others, focusing on regions **where** digital infrastructure had been established early and periods **when** government incentives were most generous. （复杂 - where/when/why 并用）

> [!warning] 易错提醒
> - **where** 不一定只用于地理位置，抽象"地点"也可以：
>   - I reached a point **where** I had to make a decision.（point = 抽象地点）
>   - This is the situation **where** patience is needed.（situation = 抽象地点）
> - 判断用关系代词还是关系副词的方法：
>   - 从句缺主语/宾语 → 用关系代词（who/which/that）
>   - 从句完整，缺状语 → 用关系副词（where/when/why）

> [!tip] 实战技巧
> **阅读中**：
> - 定语从句是长难句的主要来源，先找到先行词，再理解从句修饰的是哪个名词
> - 非限制性定语从句 which 指代整个句子时，先理解主句含义，再看补充信息
>
> **写作中**：
> - 用定语从句合并短句，使行文更紧凑
> - 学术写作中多用非限制性定语从句补充说明
> - "介词 + which" 结构比 "which...介词" 更正式
