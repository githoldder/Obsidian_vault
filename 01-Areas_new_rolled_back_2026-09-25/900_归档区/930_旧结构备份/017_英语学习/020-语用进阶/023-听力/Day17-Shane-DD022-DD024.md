## DD022

### 原句
**"The death toll from a powerful earthquake that struck the Indonesian island of Sumatra has risen to more than 1,100."**

### 听写要点
- 注意 death toll 的连读，risen to 的连读

### Shane 讲解要点
- **The death** → /ðə-deθ/（弱读 + 连读）
- **toll from** → /toʊl-frəm/（连读 + 弱读）
- **a powerful** → /ə-ˈpaʊ-ər-fəl/（弱读 + 连读）
- **earthquake that** → /ˈɜːrθ-kweɪk-ðət/（连读 + 弱读）
- **struck the** → /strʌk-ðə/（连读 + 弱读）
- **Indonesian island** → /ˌɪn-də-ˈniː-ʒən-ˈaɪ-lənd/（连读）
- **of Sumatra** → /əv-suː-ˈmɑː-trə/（弱读 + 连读）
- **has risen** → /həz-ˈrɪz-ən/（弱读 + 连读）
- **to more** → /tə-mɔːr/（弱读 + 连读）
- **than one** → /ðən-ˈwʌn/（弱读 + 连读）

### 语音现象识别
- [ ] 连读：death toll, toll from, earthquake that, struck the, Indonesian island, of Sumatra, has risen, to more, than one
- [ ] 弱读：the, a, that, the, of, has, to, than

## DD023

### 原句
**"The president is expected to announce his new economic team today, including his choice for Treasury Secretary."**

### 听写要点
- 注意 expected to 的连读，including his 的连读

### Shane 讲解要点
- **The president** → /ðə-ˈprez-ɪ-dənt/（弱读 + 连读）
- **is expected** → /ɪz-ɪkˈspek-tɪd/（连读）
- **to announce** → /tə-əˈnaʊns/（弱读 + 连读）
- **his new** → /ɪz-nu/（弱读 + 连读）
- **economic team** → /ˌiː-kəˈnɑː-mɪk-tiːm/（连读）
- **today including** → /təˈdeɪ-ɪnˈkluː-dɪŋ/（连读）
- **his choice** → /ɪz-tʃɔɪs/（弱读 + 连读）
- **for Treasury** → /fər-ˈtreʒ-əri/（弱读 + 连读）

### 语音现象识别
- [ ] 连读：is expected, to announce, his new, economic team, today including, his choice, for Treasury
- [ ] 弱读：the, is, to, his, for

## DD024

### 原句
**"The secretary general of NATO has expressed concern about the possibility of Russian military intervention in Ukraine."**

### 听写要点
- 注意 secretary general 的连读，possibility of 的连读

### Shane 讲解要点
- **The secretary** → /ðə-ˈsek-rə-teri/（弱读 + 连读）
- **general of** → /ˈdʒen-ər-əl-əv/（连读 + 弱读）
- **NATO has** → /ˈneɪ-toʊ-hæz/（连读）
- **expressed concern** → /ɪkˈsprest-kənˈsɜːrn/（连读）
- **about the** → /əˈbaʊt-ðə/（连读 + 弱读）
- **possibility of** → /ˌpɑː-səˈbɪ-lɪ-ti-əv/（连读 + 弱读）
- **Russian military** → /ˈrʌʃ-ən-ˈmɪ-lɪ-teri/（连读）
- **intervention in** → /ˌɪn-tərˈven-ʃən-ɪn/（连读）

### 语音现象识别
- [ ] 连读：secretary general, NATO has, expressed concern, about the, possibility of, Russian military, intervention in
- [ ] 弱读：the, of, about, the, of, in
